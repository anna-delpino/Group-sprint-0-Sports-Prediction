Description of data:

In Fantasy Premier League, you build your own team from real players who play in the Premier League. Each player earns you points based off of their performance in real life and certain actions in the game. For example your player can earn points by scoring a goal but will lose points if they recieve a red card. This data set includes all players registered into each teams' squad for the 2025/26 Premier League season however I have cleaned the data by removing any player with 0 minutes played for that season. I have also added an extra column to show which team each player plays for, as this wasn't included in original dataset. We can see the total points earned over the entire season for each individual player and there is also other information displayed for each player such as minutes played or yellow cards revieved.
