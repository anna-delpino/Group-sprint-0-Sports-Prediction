Description of data:

In Fantasy Premier League, you build your own team from real players who play in the Premier League. Each player earns you points based off of their performance in real life and certain actions in the game. For example your player can earn points by scoring a goal but will lose points if they recieve a red card. This data set includes all players registered into each teams' squad for the 2025/26 Premier League season. We can see the total points earned over the entire season for each individual player. There is also other information displayed for each player such as minutes played or yellow cards revieved.
